House Washing

A low-pressure soft wash that lifts algae, grime and cobwebs off your siding without forcing water where it doesn't belong.

Why It's Worth Doing

  • Algae eats into siding. The green and black streaks on North Shore homes are living organisms that degrade the surface they sit on.
  • High pressure wrecks siding. Blasting a house forces water behind panels and into wall cavities. Soft washing is the correct method.
  • Paint lasts longer. Washing off organic growth adds years before you need to repaint.
  • The difference is dramatic. Most people think their siding has faded. Usually it's just dirty.

How We Do It

1

Protect the landscaping

Plants and gardens get soaked down before and after so nothing takes on cleaning solution.

2

Apply the soft-wash mix

A biodegradable solution is applied at low pressure and left to break down the algae and grime chemically, not by force.

3

Gentle rinse

Everything gets rinsed at pressure safe for your siding type, whether vinyl, stucco, wood, brick or metal.

4

Detail the finish

Eaves, trim, light fixtures and cobwebbed corners get hand attention before we call it done.

What's Included

Full siding wash, eaves and trim, and cobweb removal. We adjust the approach to your siding material. Roof washing can be added if there's moss up top, which is worth doing at the same visit.
