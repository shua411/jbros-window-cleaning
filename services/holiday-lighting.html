Holiday Lighting

Pro-grade install, takedown and storage. Best looking house on the block, and you never touch a ladder in December.

Why It's Worth Doing

  • Ladders in December are genuinely dangerous. Icy rungs, wet roofs and cold hands are how people end up in emergency rooms every year.
  • It actually looks professional. Lights cut to your exact roofline sit straight and even, not sagging and mismatched.
  • Nothing gets damaged. We use proper clips, never staples or nails, so your fascia and shingles stay intact.
  • January is handled. Takedown and storage are included, so you're not doing the worst part of the job yourself.

How We Do It

1

Design and measure

We measure your roofline and talk through what you want lit. Rooflines, trees, columns and railings are all options.

2

Custom-cut the strands

Lights are cut and built to your home's exact measurements, so runs end where they should with no bunching.

3

Install with proper clips

Every strand is clipped, never stapled or nailed. Timers are set so it comes on automatically at dusk.

4

Takedown and storage

In January we take everything down, label it and store it for you. Next season it goes straight back up.

What's Included

Design, custom-cut pro-grade lights, full install, timer setup, and mid-season fixes if a strand goes out. Takedown and storage in January can be included. Your own lights can be installed instead if you'd rather.
