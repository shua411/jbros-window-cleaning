Who Are We

JBros is a local crew that's cleaned 400+ homes across the North Shore, and right now we're growing faster than ever. We're looking for motivated people who want to grow with us and be part of a team where everyone contributes, improves, and wins together. We care more about your drive than your resume.

The Role

  • Knocking doors in assigned neighbourhoods and talking to homeowners face to face.
  • Explaining what we do and what it costs.
  • Booking jobs on the spot and passing the details straight to the crew.
  • Showing up on time, presentable, and representing the company well at every door.
  • Handling rejection, because there's no doubt you'll hear more no's than yes's.

Why Join

Get paid what you're worth

Commission-based pay, so you're never capped by an hourly rate. You earn on every job you book, and the guys who put the hours in do very well.

Work around your schedule

Evenings, weekends, full days in the summer. Tell us what works and we'll build around it.

We train you from zero

You'll shadow us first. What to say, how to handle a no, how to close. No experience needed.

Weekly team meetings

We get together every week to run through goals, share what's working and keep everyone sharp.
