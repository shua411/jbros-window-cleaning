IMAGES — how to add your photos (no coding needed)
==================================================

Save your photos into THIS folder with the exact filenames below and
the site picks them up automatically the next time you refresh the
page. That's it — no code edits required for any of these:

  LOGO
    images/logo.png                 your round JBros logo
                                    (export it as a PNG with a
                                    TRANSPARENT background, not white —
                                    it sits on a black site and glows blue)
                                    Appears automatically in the header
                                    and footer once the file exists.

  HERO BACKGROUND (top of the site)
    images/hero.jpg                 a wide photo (a house you've cleaned,
                                    action shot, etc). It shows dimmed
                                    behind the heading + quote form with a
                                    blue glow, and fades to black as you
                                    scroll. Appears automatically once
                                    the file exists. ~1900px+ wide is ideal.

  FOUNDERS PHOTO (About section)
    images/founders.jpg             a photo of Joshua & Jayden
                                    (landscape / wider than tall works best)

  SERVICE CARDS
    images/service-exterior.jpg     Exterior window cleaning
    images/service-interior.jpg     Interior window cleaning
    images/service-pressure.jpg     Pressure washing
    images/service-housewash.jpg    House washing
    images/service-gutter.jpg       Gutter cleaning
    images/service-solar.jpg        Solar panel cleaning

  BEFORE / AFTER SLIDERS (3 of them)
    images/before-1.jpg + after-1.jpg    slider 1 (windows)
    images/before-2.jpg + after-2.jpg    slider 2 (pressure wash)
    images/before-3.jpg + after-3.jpg    slider 3 (house wash)
  (each before/after pair should be the SAME spot/angle so the
   drag reveal lines up)

NOTE on the service cards & sliders: those spots currently show stock
photos as placeholders. The logo and founders photo need no extra step —
but for the service/before-after spots, after saving your file you (or
I) also delete the stock URL from index.html so yours shows instead.
Easiest: add your files, then just tell Claude "swap in my local
images" and it's a 30-second change.

HOW TO SAVE FILES HERE
----------------------
1. Open this folder in Finder:  Jbros Website → images
2. Drag your photo in (from Photos, Downloads, AirDrop, etc.)
3. Click its name and rename it EXACTLY as listed above
   (lowercase, .jpg or .png as shown)
4. Refresh the site in your browser

Recommended: wide landscape photos, ~1200px+ wide, well-lit.
