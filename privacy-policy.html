Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

JBros Window Cleaning (“JBros”, “we”, “us”) is a residential exterior cleaning company based in North Vancouver, British Columbia. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit www.jbroswc.ca or fill out one of our forms, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA) and Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA).

1. Information you give us

When you request a quote or claim an offer, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your general location (for example, North Vancouver or West Vancouver)
  • The service you’re interested in
  • Anything else you choose to tell us, such as your address or details about your home

2. Information collected automatically

When you visit our site, some information is collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies, including your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, the link or ad that brought you here, and the date and time of your visit.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you by phone, text or email about the quote or service you requested
  • To schedule, provide and follow up on our services
  • To keep a record of enquiries and customers so we can serve you properly
  • To measure how well our advertising works and to show our ads to people more likely to be interested in our services
  • To keep our website secure and working properly

By submitting a form, you agree that we may contact you about your request. We will not add you to marketing emails without your consent, and you can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

4. Meta Pixel and Conversions API

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram, which are run by Meta Platforms, Inc. (“Meta”). Our website uses the Meta Pixel and Meta’s Conversions API. These tools tell Meta when someone visits our pages or submits a quote request after seeing one of our ads, so we can measure our ads and help Meta show them to people likely to be interested.

  • The Meta Pixel uses cookies in your browser to record page visits and form submissions.
  • When you submit a quote request, we may also send Meta your email address, phone number, name and city. This information is hashed (converted into a scrambled code) before it is sent, so Meta can match it to a Meta account without receiving it in readable form. We also send your IP address and browser information so Meta can match the visit.
  • Meta may use this information as described in its own Privacy Policy, including to provide measurement services and to target ads.

We do not sell your personal information, and we never share sensitive information such as health or financial details with Meta.

5. Your choices about ads and cookies

  • Manage how Meta uses information about you for ads in your Facebook Ad Preferences, including the setting for activity from other businesses.
  • Opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies at YourAdChoices.ca (Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada).
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Our site will still work, but our ads may be less relevant to you.

6. Who we share information with

We only share your information with service providers that help us run our business, and only as needed:

  • Meta, for advertising measurement and delivery, as described above
  • Notion, where we keep our customer and enquiry records
  • FormSubmit and Google (Gmail), which deliver form submissions to our inbox
  • Vercel, which hosts our website

Some of these providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and customer information for as long as we need it to provide our services, follow up with you and meet legal or tax obligations. When we no longer need it, we delete it.

8. Keeping it safe

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, including secure (HTTPS) connections and access-controlled accounts. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted, and you can withdraw your consent to being contacted at any time. Email us at jbroswc@gmail.com and we’ll respond within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied with our response, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for BC.

10. Children

Our services are for homeowners and residents, and our website isn’t directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
