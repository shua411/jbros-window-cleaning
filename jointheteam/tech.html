The Role

  • Cleaning windows, gutters, siding and driveways on homes across the North Shore.
  • Running our equipment: water-fed poles, the gutter vacuum, pressure washers and ladders.
  • Working safely at heights and keeping every job site clean and tidy.
  • Treating every home like it's your own, and being friendly and professional with homeowners.
  • Showing up on time, ready to work, as part of a tight crew.

What We're Looking For

  • Full-time availability. This is a full-time role, not something to fit around another job.
  • Comfortable on ladders and working outdoors in all kinds of weather.
  • Reliable, punctual and easy to work with.
  • A strong work ethic and an eye for detail. Experience helps, but we'll train you.
